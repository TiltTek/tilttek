# Credits

The library implements practices of the tabletop and live-action role-playing communities. Its texts and its code are
its own: it copies no tool's text, and describes each in its own words. It names the people each tool comes from, as
the sources below credit them; `SafetyTool::credit()` returns the same names.

| Tool | Credited to | Source | Licence of the reference text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lines and veils | Ron Edwards, in *Sex & Sorcery* (Adept Press, 2003) | the credits of the *TTRPG Safety Toolkit* | commercial book, no open licence |
| X-card | John Stavropoulos | [the X-card document](http://tinyurl.com/x-card-rpg) | CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported |
| Script Change | Beau Jágr Sheldon | [briebeau.com/scriptchange](http://briebeau.com/scriptchange) | CC BY 4.0 |
| Consent checklist | Sean K. Reynolds and Shanna Germain, the *RPG Consent Checklist* of *Consent in Gaming* (Monte Cook Games, 2019) | [montecookgames.com](https://www.montecookgames.com/consent-in-gaming/) | free PDF, no open licence |
| Open door | Eirik Fatland | the credits of the *TTRPG Safety Toolkit* | — |
| Stars and wishes | Lu Quade | [gauntlet-rpg.com](https://www.gauntlet-rpg.com/blog/stars-and-wishes) | no licence stated |
| Session zero | the tabletop role-playing community, no single author | — | — |
| Check-ins | the independent tabletop and Nordic larp communities, no single author | — | — |

The library reuses none of these texts, so no licence above binds its code. An application that prints or quotes the
text of a tool follows that tool's licence: the X-card and Script Change ask for a credit and a link to the documents
above.

The *TTRPG Safety Toolkit* of Kienna Shaw and Lauren Bryant-Monk gathers most of these tools and credits their authors;
an application that teaches them to its players may point to it.
